Turn this rough idea into a script for a short video.

Idea: [one or two sentences]
Viewer: [who is watching and what they want]
Length: [for example, 90 seconds]

Write:
1. A hook for the first five seconds. It must state a problem or a surprise, not a greeting.
2. A script written for speaking. Short sentences, contractions, no jargon the viewer would not use.
3. On-screen text for each key point, six words or fewer.
4. A closing line that gives the viewer one thing to do next.
5. Mark where cuts or visuals change.

Use US English. Do not invent statistics or examples. If the idea lacks a concrete example, ask me for one.
